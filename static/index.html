How the decision is calculated

1

AI demand forecast

Random Forest forecasts near-term demand from historical sales patterns, recent demand, day-of-week behaviour, SKU identity, and promotion status.

2

Expected sales before expiry

The forecast is projected across the batch's remaining shelf-life window.

3

Potential surplus

Scenario inventory minus expected sales before expiry. Negative values are treated as zero.

4

Waste-risk assessment

Surplus exposure is weighed together with remaining shelf life to assign a risk level.

5

Recommended intervention

The system surfaces a recommendation for a human inventory manager to review — it never executes an action on its own.

ℹ️ Prototype scope & responsible AI

InventoryWise AI is a decision-support prototype built on synthetic/public benchmark FMCG sales data.

Product shelf life and batch-level inventory exposure are simulated for demonstration, because the source sales dataset doesn't contain reliable batch-level expiry records.

The AI component forecasts demand — it does not directly predict food waste. Potential surplus and waste-risk outputs come from combining that forecast with inventory and shelf-life assumptions.

Every recommendation is intended for human review before operational use.
